HOW I THINK
& SOLVE PROBLEMS.

Great software engineering is not merely writing syntax—it is understanding user needs, debugging with scientific rigor, and continually connecting the dots between technologies.

01

Root-Cause Debugging & Troubleshooting

I do not apply temporary band-aids. Whether it is an insidious race condition in client-side state, a bottleneck in database indexing, or a CSS subgrid misalignment, I dive deep into DevTools profilers, server logs, and network waterfalls until the fundamental cause is eradicated.

02

Learning by Connecting Concepts

I firmly believe that learning is about making connections. Having mastered backend architectures and database modeling early on, adopting reactive frontend frameworks and modern AI tooling felt natural because the core architectural paradigms intersect and reinforce one another.

03

AI-Augmented Velocity, Human Judgment

I leverage LLMs (Cursor, Claude, Copilot) as supercharged intellectual co-pilots for boilerplate elimination, rapid exploration, and synthetic testing. However, architectural design, type safety, security auditing, and UX refinement always remain under strict human ownership.

04

Ownership, Transparency & Delivery

From high-level discussions with product stakeholders to writing clean commit logs and setting up reliable deployments, I treat every project with founder-level ownership. When commitments are made, deadlines are met without excuses.
