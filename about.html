Mental Model

My Learning Philosophy: Connecting The Dots

I firmly believe that true engineering mastery comes from connecting concepts. Over the years, engineers gather fragments of knowledge in isolation. But true breakthroughs occur when you connect those ideas: linking how a database stores indexed B-trees to how a browser schedules DOM repaints, or understanding how declarative UI states map to relational tables.

Every time I evaluate a new language, framework, or AI paradigm, I look for its underlying connections to first principles. This allows me to pick up new technologies and deliver production code in days rather than months.
